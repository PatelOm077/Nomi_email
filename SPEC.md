# Nomi — Spec

One-line promise: "Install it, and your store's email is done."
AI email app embedded in Shopify admin. Merchant installs → AI reads
their store (logo, colors, products, brand voice) → every notification
and marketing email is branded, written, and live in about a minute.
Templates are AI-generated per merchant, not picked from a library.

## The five launch features (v1 — nothing else)

1. Magic setup — AI brands and writes every email from the storefront.
   Done = on install, the app reads the store and produces branded,
   written emails for all core types without manual setup.

2. Order/notification emails — order confirmation, shipping, refunds.
   Sent natively by Shopify from installed templates.
   Done = a real order renders a branded, AI-written confirmation with
   real customer name, real line items, real total.
   [status: order confirmation generating from a live order ✓]

3. Abandoned cart recovery — works out of the box.
   Done = a cart with items generates a branded recovery email.

4. One-prompt newsletters — describe a campaign, get a send-ready email.
   Done = free-text prompt ("Diwali sale, 15% off") → branded campaign email.

5. Review requests — post-delivery follow-up.
   Done = a delivered order generates a branded review-request email.

## Cross-cutting properties (apply to all five, not features themselves)
- Every language: 7–10 international languages, generated by the same model.
- One flat price: bill never scales with subscriber/contact count.

## Explicitly OUT of scope for v1
SMS. Popups. Complex segmentation. Settings sprawl. A browsable library of
generic (non-branded) templates. When tempted to add: "not yet."

## Choice principle
Never remove choice, remove effort. Merchant chooses which look / whether
to go seasonal; Nomi does the work. Options shown are always rendered in
the merchant's own brand, never generic.

## Roadmap (post-v1, not now)
Seasonal auto-refresh (Christmas/BFCM/Diwali "remix"), AI product
recommendations per customer, "Looks" gallery (archetypes in merchant's
brand), revenue attribution dashboard, multi-platform (WooCommerce → Wix
→ BigCommerce; keep engine platform-independent).
